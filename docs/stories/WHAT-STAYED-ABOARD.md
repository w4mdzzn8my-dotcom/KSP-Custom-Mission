# Routine scene: What stayed aboard

**Status:** proposed cooperative data-review scene, requiring actual stored reports
for any measured result. It is not a playable mission trigger.

Basil captures the retained-report baseline at the science station. Hamish reads
back the vessel, reference body and timestamp before they review another capture.
The screen keeps the experiment request separate from the stored quantities.

“Put both times in the handover,” Hamish says.

“And the subject,” Basil adds. “Ada should be able to tell what we compared.”

If the inventory is unchanged or empty, they record that observation. If a subject
total changes, they check the actual containers and recent transfers before
attributing a cause. They leave the reports untouched for the next review.

This is routine pilot/engineer cooperation. It assumes no science reward,
experiment completion, unique discovery, automatic transmission or extra
emergency. Stock experiment execution still follows its separate role and
result-dialog requirements.
