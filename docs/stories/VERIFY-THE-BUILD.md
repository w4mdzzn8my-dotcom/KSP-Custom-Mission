# Routine handover: verify the build label

At the morning console, Hamish reads the pinned campaign version and overlay revisions from the manifest while Basil checks the integrated candidate’s manifest tree and plugin SHA-256 against the intended QA copy. They label cryopod, lander, and node runs **pending** until the matching disposable save and raw runtime evidence exist. The handover records what was actually compiled and staged, then leaves every gameplay result blank until someone observes it; matching names or a completed source build are not counted as a passed test.

Proposed ordinary handover only; the player-written record creates no automatic
mission achievement, animated meeting or additional emergency.
