# Routine scene: One interval

**Status:** proposed systems-review conversation using actual survey observations;
no playable trigger, animated meeting or completed engineering objective is claimed.

Hamish starts a read-only power survey during the human watch. Basil compares
the recorded charge, capacity and device configuration with the operations notes.
They wait for accepted samples while continuing their ordinary instrument review.

“It barely moved,” Hamish says.

“Put the level beside the change,” Basil replies. “And both times.”

If the store was full, they note that the flat reading cannot reveal surplus
generation. If it was empty, they check the actual supply situation rather than
calling the flat line a balance. A changed reading remains an observation of
that interval, with no automatic conclusion about the responsible device.

Their player-written handover carries the measured window and its limits to
the relief watch. Nothing in the dialogue certifies future endurance, cryopod
power, a repaired fault or an extra emergency.
